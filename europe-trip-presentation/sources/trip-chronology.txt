EUROPE BICYCLE TRIP: VERIFIED CHRONOLOGY

Scope
-----
The trip ran from June 24 through July 10, 2026: 14 riding days and three
non-riding days. Kian crossed seven European countries: Germany, France,
Switzerland, Austria, Liechtenstein, Belgium, and the Netherlands.

Distance
--------
Use 3,049.438 km as the preferred cycling estimate. The completed local audit
places the plausible range at 3,040-3,061 km and excludes all train and car
movement. The YouTube series rounds this to 3,000 km; its summary narration
calls the route roughly 3,100 km. The older 2,780 km figure in the running notes
was a placeholder and is superseded by the activity-, route-, and photo-backed
audit.

Calendar and ride ledger
------------------------
Jun 22 | Departure setup
  Left at 3:00 a.m., packed the bicycle into a cardboard box, and had an
  emotional goodbye with Kian's mother. Source: Obsidian 2026-06-22.

Jun 23 | Arrival in Germany
  Arrived near Frankfurt, rebuilt the bike in 33 C heat, and discovered that
  the headset would not stay tight. Vlog Day 1, 00:23-04:46.

Jun 24 | Ride 1 | Mittelbuchen -> Karlsruhe | 224.37 km (confidence B)
  The pedal worked loose and destroyed the crank threads. Kian pedaled one-footed
  to a station, took the Kelsterbach-Mainz S-Bahn, tried four shops, repaired the
  bike, and still reached Karlsruhe around 10 p.m. The train is excluded from the
  cycling total. Vlog Day 1, 05:03-11:57.

Jun 25 | Ride 2 | Karlsruhe -> Heidolsheim, Alsace | 157.57 km (B)
  Rode the Rhine in heat reported near 42 C, navigated from EuroVelo signs after
  losing the Polar charger, and crossed into France. Vlog Day 2, 00:00-05:37.

Jun 26 | Ride 3 | Heidolsheim -> Lottstetten | 202.55 km (A)
  Reached the France-Germany-Switzerland tri-border, nearly lost the passport,
  discovered Switzerland's public fountains, and finished back across the
  German border. Vlog Day 3, 03:47-15:41.

Jun 27 | Ride 4 | Lottstetten -> Danis | 278.06 km (B)
  Visited Rhine Falls, crossed Germany, Switzerland, Austria, and Liechtenstein
  in one day, then forced a late 100 km push through the Alps. A thunderstorm
  soaked the bike and killed the phone. Vlog Day 4, 00:00-15:45.

Jun 28 | Rest 1 | Near Danis | 0 km
  Sunday closures left Kian stranded without a working phone. He hiked with
  Marika and met Jerome at the hostel. Vlog Day 5, 00:19-01:59 and 09:37-11:20.

Jun 29 | Ride 5 | Danis -> Brig; train to Sion | 135.60 km (A)
  Crossed Oberalp Pass (about 2,000 m) and Furka Pass (2,436 m; a 13 km climb
  with about 890 m gain), then descended toward the Rhone. Rain and a failed
  booking led to a 40-minute Brig-Sion train, excluded from cycling distance.
  Vlog Day 5, 01:59-13:26; Vlog Day 6, 00:01-03:07.

Jun 30 | Ride 6 | Sion -> Geneva; train to Valserhone | 163.87 km (A/B)
  Followed the Rhone and the south shore of Lake Geneva through Evian. Both
  Airbnb bookings canceled, the backup phone died, and Kian was panicking at
  Geneva station until Zahran offered him a place to sleep. The train into
  Valserhone is excluded. Vlog Day 6, 03:25-17:03; Vlog Day 7, 00:01-03:53.

Jul 1 | Ride 7 | Valserhone -> Lagnieu/Amberieu | 140.66 km (B/C)
  The entire activity is missing; the audit reconstructs it from fifteen photo
  and endpoint constraints. A Carrefour repair shop replaced the phone screen
  for EUR 180. Kian then learned the original loop would require 264 km every
  day plus the Italian Alps, and decided to reroute north through Paris.
  Vlog Day 7, 04:08-17:27.

Jul 2 | Ride 8 | Lagnieu -> Fleurville via Lyon | 172.48 km (A)
  A front puncture forced a 20 km limp to a Giant shop: repeated hand-pumping,
  no turns, weight shifted backward, and two liters of water dumped. Olivier
  and Anton replaced the tire, charged only EUR 50 for the part, and gave away
  the labor and tire levers. Kian revisited Lyon, then followed the Saone north.
  Vlog Day 8, 00:00-19:32.

Jul 3 | Ride 9 | Fleurville -> Nevers | 274.98 km (A)
  Rode the Voie Bleue/EuroVelo 6 on baguettes and bananas. A wasp sting, a
  nosebleed, a lost Croc elastic, a headwind, and an 80 km/h road without a
  shoulder made the final hours ugly. Arrival: about 9:45 p.m.
  YouTube upload is labeled Day 10, 00:01-08:58.

Jul 4 | Ride 10 | Nevers -> Paris | 287.47 km (B)
  Started hungry before stores opened, followed the Loire past a nuclear plant,
  passed Fontainebleau, then entered Paris through traffic and confusion. Apple
  Maps sent Kian 3 km from the right address; he reached his aunt's home around
  11:30 p.m. as neighborhoods watched France play in the World Cup.
  YouTube upload is labeled Day 11, 00:01-08:53.

Jul 5 | Rest 2 | Paris | 0 km
  Spent the day with Kian's aunt. Source: Weekly Victories and Obsidian.

Jul 6 | Ride 11 | Paris -> Drocourt | 206.39 km (A)
  Trimmed a planned 280 km route to 200 km, left Paris, ate his aunt's hard-boiled
  eggs while riding, passed World War I cemeteries and poppy fields, and reached
  the northern coal country to reunite with Jean-Luc.
  YouTube upload is labeled Day 9, 00:01-07:45.

Jul 7 | Rest 3 | Drocourt and Vimy | 0 km
  Spent the day with Jean-Luc; the Vimy outing was by car and is excluded from
  bicycle mileage. The buffet appears at the end of the upload labeled Day 9,
  07:45-08:09, and the following episode reports the resulting stomachache.

Jul 8 | Ride 12 | Drocourt -> Bruges plus city loop | 197.17 km (A)
  Rode Paris-Roubaix cobbles into Belgium, visited Tournai, fought a visible
  headwind, and found Ghent's bicycle infrastructure. After reaching Bruges,
  Kian unloaded the bike and rode a 16.06 km city loop. With 600 km remaining,
  he chose two 300 km days over three 200 km days to see family sooner.
  Vlog Day 12, 00:01-08:56.

Jul 9 | Ride 13 | Bruges -> Cologne | 337.84 km (A/B)
  Left before 6 a.m., stopped at a Canadian war cemetery, crossed the 31 m-deep
  Sint-Anna tunnel, then passed through the Netherlands and Germany. The final
  hours were ridden in darkness while holding a phone flashlight. It remains
  the trip's longest and hardest day. Vlog Day 13, 00:00-06:53.

Jul 10 | Ride 14 | Cologne -> Mittelbuchen/Hanau | 270.42 km (A/B)
  Saw Cologne Cathedral, Bonn, and the Rhine, but began the day with dead legs
  after the 337 km ride. The final two days totaled about 608 km by the audit
  (the vlog estimated 566 km before reconstruction). Kian crossed the Rhine for
  the last time and arrived just after 11 p.m.; family had tracked the phone and
  waited in the driveway. Obsidian ends the day: "I LOVE LIFE SO MUCH."
  Vlog Day 14, 00:01-08:41.

Jul 14 and Jul 27 | Epilogue
  Back in San Francisco, the damaged bike box had lost both pedals and the tool
  kit. Seventeen days after the finish, hand blisters and numb toes remained.
  Vlog Day 14, 08:41-13:04; Summary, 04:30-05:55.
