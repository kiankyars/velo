# California coast hiker/biker campsites

Quick reference for the California coast in both directions from San Francisco.
Campground facts and operator pages were checked **11 August 2026**.

> **Live Big Sur warning (11 August 2026):** the
> [Timber Fire](https://www.fire.ca.gov/incidents/2026/8/9/timber-fire/) has
> active evacuation orders and all Big Sur-area state parks are closed. Andrew
> Molera, Pfeiffer Big Sur and Limekiln are therefore not usable now. Plaskett
> Creek's current federal notice also says Highway 1 is closed from Monterey.
> Recheck the incident and road status before the later Los Angeles trip.

## Route and distance basis

- The backbone is the official [USBR 95 Ride with GPS
  collection](https://ridewithgps.com/collections/11043144) from the U.S.
  Bicycle Route System account.
- That collection currently includes the
  [North-Central southbound track](https://ridewithgps.com/routes/46641093), but
  accidentally omits the matching official
  [North-Central northbound track](https://ridewithgps.com/routes/46362440).
  Use the northbound file going out and the southbound file returning.
- Golden Gate Bridge south portal to the **CA-1/US-101 junction at Leggett** is
  **352.77 km northbound**; the official return track is **352.40 km**. The
  official-track round trip is **705.17 km**, before the connector to 426 Fell.
- This Golden Gate Bridge–Leggett subset uses **no US-101 before the Leggett
  endpoint**. It uses Marin roads and paths, then CA-1 from Point Reyes Station.
  [Caltrans describes the same corridor](https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-1/d1-popular-links/d1-bicycle-tourism).
- Distances below are rounded official-route kilometres from the Golden Gate
  Bridge to the closest point on the track. They **exclude campground access
  detours**, so off-route or backcountry access is called out separately.
- `HB` means the operator currently confirms a human-powered campsite. Unless a
  park says otherwise, California State Parks says HB sites are normally
  per-person and first-come/first-served. `Call` means the operator does not
  currently publish the HB price; it is deliberately not replaced with an old
  forum price.

## What was wrong in the supplied table

- Its north-coast distances increasingly undercounted the official route:
  MacKerricher is about **288 km**, not 260 km, and the no-101 Leggett junction
  is **353 km**, not 290 km, from the Golden Gate Bridge.
- Most of the repeated `$5` and `$7` figures were old or unsupported. Unpublished
  fees are marked `Call` instead of guessed.
- "Bull Point" was removed; Stillwater Cove is the actual county campground.
- Point Reyes Coast and Wildcat are reserved backcountry trips, not spontaneous
  roadside HB sites.
- Plaskett Creek, El Chorro, Pismo and Dockweiler are not dependable HB stops;
  Dockweiler explicitly prohibits tent camping.
- Richardson Grove through Jedediah Smith are north of the CA-1/US-101 junction
  and therefore outside the no-US-101 trip.
